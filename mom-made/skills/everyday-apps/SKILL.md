# Defaults for everyday apps

- Make the first screen the useful one: the list, the form or the answer. No landing page unless asked.
- Use the person's real wording and content. Ask one short question if something essential is missing, otherwise make a sensible choice and say what you chose.
- Keep it small and finished: one clear main action, large tap targets, readable text, works on a phone.
- Typical shapes: tracker (add entry, see history, simple totals), list or checklist (add, tick off, delete with undo), shared page (read-only view people can open), sign-up or RSVP (form, thank-you message, a list only the owner should see), tool or calculator (inputs, clear result, copy button).
- Pages meant to be shared should have a good title, a short description and a friendly look when opened from a link on a phone.
- Anything the owner should see but visitors should not needs sign-in or Cloudflare Access. Follow the auth guidance and say plainly what is public.
- No fake data, fake testimonials or placeholder text left in the finished app.
- Do not add features that were not asked for. Offer one or two ideas in your reply instead.
