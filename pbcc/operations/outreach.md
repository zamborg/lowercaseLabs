## Questions for member conversations

- Which two or three driving experiences are essential? What would you personally use, rather than just enjoy seeing in the garage?
- How many weekdays, weekend blocks, and miles would you actually want? How far ahead can you plan? Which holidays or seasons matter?
- How far would you travel to pick up a car, and must pickup be self-service?
- Is this primarily community, access, shared collecting, or something else for you?
- What's your acceptable all-in annual operating cost, including usage and assessments? How does $400–$500 monthly feel alongside the capital contribution, and what would you expect that to include?
- What booking limits feel fair when everyone wants the same 911? Would you accept a waitlist or rotating draft?
- What exit notice, valuation uncertainty, and resale delay could you tolerate? Could you withstand a further repair assessment or capital loss?
- Would you help with physical operations, or expect a fully paid concierge service? Do not assume volunteer work until someone explicitly commits.

Record answers with pseudonymous IDs, interest stage (curious / exploring / conditional interest / documented commitment), concerns, and next step. Verbal enthusiasm is not a subscription or capital commitment. Collect driver-sensitive information only through the appropriate later onboarding process.
