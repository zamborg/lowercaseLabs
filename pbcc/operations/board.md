## Weekly COO brief format

1. Current stage and change since last review.
2. The most consequential decision needed from the founder, with recommendation and evidence.
3. Money: verified cash if available, projected expenses/funding, reserve position, and model changes. Until banking exists, say “planning figures.”
4. Member demand, vendor responses, overdue promises, and dependencies.
5. Fleet readiness, maintenance/incident exceptions, and access fairness once operating.
6. Concrete next actions with owners and dates; separate drafts from sent/committed actions.
