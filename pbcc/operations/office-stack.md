# PBCC office plan

November 17, 2026 · The founder owns relationships and decisions. The project files and skills support preparation, records, and follow-up. Build around a small group that already trusts each other and can handle simple handovers.

| Workstream | First useful version | Accountable owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Front office** · what members see | A private tour kit: club pamphlet, one page on what a member is committing to, and a page per candidate car with its role, cost range, alternatives and access tradeoffs. Track interest and feedback in a private roster. Add a private booking view once the rules have been tested. | Founder owns the tour; COO drafts materials and consolidates feedback. |
| **Admin** · how the club runs | A private roster of members and separately approved drivers, fleet vote record, shared calendar, and one issue log covering handovers, vehicle availability, service holds, and contested-weekend decisions. Start with a named coordinator; members handle routine handovers. | Founder designates an operator; COO prepares checklists and exception reviews. |
| **Back office** · what the club must fund and honor | An operating budget and reserve policy; evidence on entity, title and coverage; storage and service terms; cash and bookkeeping procedures; a record of who can approve repairs, claims, assessments, transfers, and exits. | Founder owns commitments; counsel, accountant, and broker confirm specifics; COO maintains the evidence and decisions. |

Start by drafting the tour kit privately while validating coverage and garage/service costs for the two fleet sizes. Use a single version of the terms across the pamphlet and candidate pages. The commitment page should explain what the proposed $50,000 buys, how dues and any use charges work, what access and exit would look like, and which answers are still pending. Before presenting firm terms, choose a funded fleet and minimum cohort and pass the board's recruiting gate. The founder can have discovery conversations in the meantime.

Run a calendar and ledger rehearsal before specifying booking software: two people want the same headline car for the same weekend, then the car goes into service. Check who makes each decision, which records change, and what members see. Use the observed friction to scope any portal; the initial member view can stay as small as availability, a request, and its status. Keep driver documents, insurance files, and payment records in restricted storage, with redacted status references in this workspace.

Use the existing [operating board](board.md) as the shared queue, the [outreach drafts](outreach.md) for quote discovery, and the [formation agenda](../docs/formation.md) for adviser work. The [founder handoff](../docs/founder-handoff.md) records what's available from the November briefing and what still needs reconciliation.
