# Formation and coverage discovery agenda

November 8, 2026 · Questions for advisers, not adopted legal terms.

## Give advisers one consistent description

Proposed California Peninsula member-owned car collection; 8–10 economic members at $50,000 each; four initial cars; revised working monthly dues of $400–$500, potentially usage charges; member-led coordination preferred, with any paid management scope undecided; secure common garage, with AutoKeep Menlo Park a preferred inquiry lead; approved members reserve cars for recreational road driving. No public rentals, track use, guest drivers, or interstate permissions are assumed in the pilot. Confirm these boundaries with the founder and disclose any changes to advisers and the insurer.

Specify expected approved-driver count separately from owners, prospective driver profiles, actual VINs when selected, annual mileage, overnight storage away from the garage, pickup/return arrangements, and whether employees or vendors move cars. Unknown facts must stay unknown in submissions.

## Counsel agenda

- Compare an LLC, a cooperative legal form, and other appropriate structures against the intended economics. Decide who legally owns/titles vehicles and who contracts with members. “Co-op” is not yet a legal classification.
- Explain exposure for the driving member, non-driving owners, manager, and vehicle-owning entity; negligent entrustment and operational failures; limits of entity protection; waivers, indemnities, and personal guarantees. Do not promise equal liability or complete protection from using an entity.
- Determine whether membership sales or any return/appreciation language creates securities issues; what private recruitment is permissible; what offering or membership disclosures are appropriate. Do this before circulating firm terms or taking funds.
- Define capital accounts/equity, votes, fee recipient and services, conflicts and founder compensation, admission, transfer, divorce/death/disability, defaults, suspension, expulsion, assessments, and dissolution.
- Design exits: valuation method, independent pricing/dispute process, transfer approval, buyer search, notice, payout timing, liquidity limits, and when asset sales could be required. Avoid a cash redemption promise the club cannot fund.
- Determine whether any proposed use/fee model triggers rental, consumer, business-license, local permitting, or other obligations. A private membership label alone does not resolve classification.
- Set governance: routine delegated operations versus member votes for purchases/sales, debt, major spending, related-party transactions, changes to dues/access, new members, and additional capital. Choose thresholds in the governing documents rather than inventing authority in an agent workflow.

Deliverable: a structure recommendation, cost/timeline estimate, written issue list, and counsel-prepared formation/member documents when the founder elects to proceed.

## Insurance broker agenda

Ask the broker to confirm, in writing, the carrier/product, underwriting assumptions, exclusions, and remaining conditions. A nonbinding indication is feasibility evidence; it is not bound coverage.

- Exact insured owner/manager entities, every driver category, and paid member-use classification; confirm dues, usage charges, and management fees are accurately disclosed.
- Vehicle liability, physical damage and agreed-value basis, deductibles, UM/UIM and other applicable coverages, umbrella/excess, premises/events, and vehicle custody by shops/storage providers. Verify which policies cover which risks rather than assuming a single bundle.
- Member-driving and permissive-use terms; age/experience/record criteria; excluded drivers; additional drivers; manual-car familiarization; screening frequency and consent.
- Overnight trips, mileage limits, geography, commuting, weather, towing, out-of-state use, and any excluded track/autocross/rental activity.
- Gaps between storage, handover, driving, roadside recovery, service, transport, and return. Ask about deductibles, diminished value, total-loss settlement, salvage, and business interruption/loss of use.
- Whether each member's own policy must carry specified coverage; whether it responds at all. Never substitute an assumption about credit-card rental coverage.
- Premium, minimum earned premium, deposits, audit/true-up terms, cancellation, claims reporting, adding/replacing cars/drivers, and storage/security conditions.

Request a VIN-specific binder and policy documents before vehicle risk transfers. Review with counsel where needed. Record renewal dates and driver approval evidence before allowing access.

The [California DOI alert](https://www.insurance.ca.gov/0400-news/0102-alerts/2023/Consumer-Alert-on-Coverage-for-Rentals.cfm) explains why personal vehicle-sharing and traditional rentals cannot be treated interchangeably for coverage. It does not determine PBCC's classification. Hagerty's [business overview](https://www.hagerty.com/insurance/business-insurance) is a broker lead, not evidence that member fleet use is covered.

## Accountant / tax agenda

- Classify capital contributions, initiation/management fees, dues, refundable amounts, member assessments, usage payments, and any distributions separately.
- Confirm purchase sales/use tax treatment and actual rate; if rentals/leases apply, determine purchase-versus-receipts tax treatment and registrations. The model's 9% acquisition input is a placeholder and must not cause double counting with any tax on use receipts. Start with [CDTFA's rental guide](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/industry/rental-companies/leases-in-general.htm).
- Determine federal/state treatment, filing obligations, depreciation/basis, member reporting, any personal-use limitations, and sale/exit consequences. Do not promise deductibility or favorable treatment.
- Budget ongoing bookkeeping, returns, entity taxes/fees, payroll or contractor treatment, and manager compensation. FTB's [LLC overview](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/index.html) describes the $800 annual tax if that structure is selected; other taxes and fees may apply.
- Establish bank controls, reconciliations, member statements, records retention, and a distinct view of unrestricted cash versus earmarked reserves. No credit-card or driver data belongs in this repository.

## Garage diligence

Verify shared-club use is allowed in writing, practical driver access, approved-driver keys/codes, log retention, key replacement/revocation, insurer-required security, custody coverage/limits, fire/flood exposure, battery charging, transport access, daily-driver parking, and emergency retrieval. For an exclusive lease, have the local jurisdiction/landlord confirm permitted use and occupancy and have counsel review the lease and guarantees. Decide who is physically responsible for opening, inspections, and emergencies.

## Membership economics to settle

Proposed equal ownership does not require unlimited or equal realized use. Decide separately: equity percentages; voting rights; driver approval; booking rights; ongoing fees; wear-cost allocation; routine damage versus negligence; insured and uninsured losses; ordinary assessments versus emergency calls; founder fees; and exit pricing.

The goal is a short, understandable member summary consistent with the final adviser-prepared documents. The initial [member discovery draft](../operations/outreach.md) gathers preferences without presenting these undecided terms as commitments.
