# Working charter

Status: founder concept captured November 8, 2026. Proposals below have not been adopted as legal or membership terms.

## Founder intent

Build a small consortium that buys and holds interesting cars for members to reserve and drive. Offer the pleasure and community of a thoughtfully maintained collection with less individual storage and maintenance work. Prioritize the long term over speed to launch.

Founder clarification, November 8, 2026: this is a very exclusive internal circle of people with strong existing trust and high integrity. Think of a shared third garage for people around Portola Valley who have expensive/scarce garage space. Choose compelling, varied cars, including useful oddballs, rather than building around supercar status. Member-led handovers and light coordination are preferred; a paid concierge operation is not assumed. Trust should reduce administrative friction while preserving clear maintenance status and key custody.

## The member promise to develop

A finite group shares a varied, maintained collection under transparent cost and access rules. Members know the capital at risk, annual operating burden, booking limits, repair responsibilities, and exit mechanism before joining. Access is subject to availability, maintenance, training, and insurer approval.

Do not promise appreciation, investment returns, unlimited driving, universal driver eligibility, equal liability, or an on-demand capital refund.

## Responsibility split

**Founder:** recruit compatible members; own the vision and community; make introductions; select advisers; approve spending and contracts; close member commitments; decide policy within the eventual governance structure.

**Assistant / COO workflows:** maintain the operating board; research and compare options; model cash needs; prepare adviser questions and outreach; summarize supplied or authorized correspondence; track promises and deadlines; prepare fleet dossiers, maintenance reviews, and booking-fairness reports.

**Qualified advisers and vendors:** resolve entity, tax, insurance, facility, and vehicle-specific questions; inspect and service vehicles; confirm coverage and driver eligibility. The assistant records their actual findings and unresolved conditions.
