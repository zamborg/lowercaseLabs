Researched November 8, 2026 from provider and government pages. Published offerings are leads, not quotes, availability confirmations, endorsements, or proof of suitability for a shared fleet. Recheck before relying on a price or term.

## Which model is PBCC?

| Model | Member receives | PBCC implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member-owned collection | Economic stake plus governed access | Closest to founder intent; must solve governance, exits, operating funding, and coverage together |
| Operator-owned driving club | Access under a membership agreement | Useful service and reservation comparator; member ownership is not established by the pages below |
| Storage/social club | Space for the customer's own car plus community | Potential operating partner and recruiting context; not inherently a shared driving fleet |
| Individually owned cars shared/rented through a platform | Per-use access to someone else's car | Different ownership and coverage structure; do not assume platform economics or statutory treatment transfers |

**Classic Car Club Manhattan** advertises separate clubhouse, driving, and corporate memberships; driving membership includes fleet access. Borrow the separation of community and driving privileges, not an assumed ownership model. No current pricing is relied upon here. [Membership page](https://classiccarclubmanhattan.com/membership/).

**Auto Vivendi** publishes annual driving-day and mileage allowances, driver screening, additional-driver terms, and cancellation rules. Its UK operating and insurance context is not a California coverage template. Borrow the idea that finite capacity and eligibility need explicit rules. [Application and terms](https://www.autovivendi.com/membership-form/).

**Carrera Drivers Club** describes Peninsula car storage and social amenities; its site does not establish a member-owned rental fleet. It is a local infrastructure lead. [Official site](https://www.carreradriversclub.com/).

**Interpretation:** PBCC can occupy a smaller, more participatory position than a luxury driving club: members choose and steward a collection together. Whether that delivers value depends on all-in annual cost, access to the specific cars people want, and trust around repairs and exits. This is a strategic hypothesis, not a market-demand finding. No directly matching local member-owned four-car co-op was verified in this initial scan.

## What to buy from a service relationship

Request preventive schedules, baseline inspections, priority diagnosis, itemized estimates and authorization thresholds, labor rate/parts policy, transport arrangements, warranty terms, records access, and a monthly fleet-health view. Compare pay-as-you-go plus priority access with any retainer. A discounted hourly rate is less useful if cars sit for weeks waiting for a slot. Do not prepay large blocks of hours without knowing expiry, exclusions, refunds, and expected demand.

For storage, compare actual four-car access costs, not just rent: retrieval staffing, daily-driver spaces, keys, security, chargers, overnight returns, towing access, restrictions on group/shared use, and the operator's custody insurance. A dedicated lease adds deposits, utilities, fit-out, security, permitted use, and physical operations. Neither option is presumed cheaper until quoted.
