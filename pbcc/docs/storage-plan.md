# Storage priority and revised dues

November 8, 2026 · Planning brief; no vendor contacted or price negotiated.

Founder direction: AutoKeep's facilities are appealing, especially Menlo Park for proximity to Portola Valley. Try a group arrangement and compare alternatives. Working dues are now $400–$500/month, superseding the original $200–$400 aspiration. Preserve the small trusted group and light member-led operations when comparing options.

## Current public benchmark

[AutoKeep](https://www.theautokeep.com/) lists 3551 Haven Avenue in Menlo Park, $500/car/month for multiple indoor cars, and $675/month for indoor trucks/vans under 22 feet. It advertises retrieval by prior text notification and a daily-driver parking option. Confirm which terms and inventory apply to Menlo Park and to shared club drivers; advertised retrieval is not a direct-access agreement.

For three standard cars plus a Silverado, use **$2,175/month** as an unconfirmed rack-rate sensitivity: 3 × $500 + $675. The truck's actual category, space, charging costs, availability and multi-driver permissions require a quote. The base model retains $2,000/month as its all-standard-car comparison, not a claim that the truck receives the cheaper rate.

## Negotiating position to explore

Ask AutoKeep to price four cars as one group account with one contracting/billing contact, a defined approved-driver roster, and club-managed reservations. Seek a package including the truck and clearly specified charging, retrieval, parking, keys, security and notice requirements. Offer predictable occupancy and ask whether commitment length changes pricing, but do not promise lower retrieval volume: shared cars could move more often than individual collectors' cars.

Test **$1,600–$1,800/month for the package** as an internal negotiating aspiration, not a quoted market rate or an authorized offer. Compared with the illustrative $2,175 rack total, that saves $375–$575/month, or $4,500–$6,900/year. Avoid trading away access that members actually need for a nominal discount. No long lease, prepayment or minimum-volume commitment has been approved.

The specific [AutoKeep draft](../operations/outreach.md) asks for the provider's proposal without stating this internal target. It remains unsent.

## What storage savings actually solve

For a controlled sensitivity, start with the base model's $82,400 annual funding need, remove the $12,000/year ongoing management fee for a member-led case, and replace its $24,000/year storage line. Keep every other assumption unchanged, including unquoted insurance and repair savings:

| Storage arrangement | Annual funding need, member-led | Monthly need | Gap at 10 × $500/month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Unconfirmed rack mix, $2,175/month | $72,500 | $6,042 | $1,042/month |
| Aspirational package, $1,800/month | $68,000 | $5,667 | $667/month |
| Aspirational package, $1,600/month | $65,600 | $5,467 | $467/month |

At eight members paying $500, add $1,000/month to each gap. Removing paid management requires someone to take on the work; it is not a vendor saving. This is a sensitivity on the original base, not a new budget for a specific sample fleet. Insurance quotes, fleet choice and maintenance can materially change it. The base's one-time management fee remains separate and is not removed by this recurring-cost comparison.

This makes ten members near the upper end of the dues range more plausible, but does not yet establish break-even. Compare real quotes before promising an all-inclusive fee. Do not enlarge the group purely to solve storage costs without checking member access.

## Alternative-facility comparison

Obtain at least one comparable quote from the maintained Peninsula shortlist. Evaluate door-to-door convenience, four-space total including truck/charging, permitted shared access, pickup/return process, daily-driver parking, keys/security, deposits, exit terms and physical work the club must perform. A dedicated lease can be considered if a strong lead appears, but rent alone is not its full operating cost.
