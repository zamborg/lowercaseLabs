# PBCC fleet and access

Locate the PBCC root above this skill directory. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/charter.md`, `docs/fleet-and-reservations.md`, and `operations/board.md`. For vehicle brainstorming read `docs/sample-fleets.md` and `finance/fleet-options.json`. For the separate base budget read `finance/assumptions.json`; for coverage questions read `docs/formation.md`. Preserve the founder's preference for an exclusive trusted circle, interesting third cars, and simple member-led handovers.

Separate the workflow requested:

- **Acquisition:** evaluate a real candidate against its collection role. Research current primary listings/transactions when needed; separate asking prices from completed sales and allocation ceilings from valuations. Include inspection independence, title/history, registration feasibility, insurer acceptance, service/parts support, all-in acquisition/catch-up costs, and reserve effect. Do not infer what “K12s” meant or treat a budget ceiling as a market recommendation.
- **Readiness/maintenance:** reconcile actual service records, due dates/mileage, incidents, availability, and a responsible physical custodian. Flag a missing record rather than marking a car inspected. An agent cannot clear a safety hold based solely on calendar availability.
- **Reservations:** identify whether policies are proposed or adopted and whether supplied requests are hypothetical or live. Apply adopted rules with per-car availability, driver approval, no overlapping bookings, maintenance priority, and explicit timezone. The proposed manual pilot is a discussion starting point, not authority to book vehicles.
- **Fairness:** include denied requests, member preference fulfillment, peak blocks by car/member, short-notice use, cancellations, and downtime. Aggregate utilization alone cannot show that members have access to the car they joined for.

For simulations, state assumptions and use pseudonymous IDs. For real operations, reconcile the latest booking source before any authorized change, preserve a unique booking ID, and avoid duplicate reservations on retries. No live calendar or vehicle control system exists initially.

Recommend fleet or policy changes with their cost/access implications; update the board with evidence and owner. Purchase, disposal, spending, member admission, policy adoption, and live booking changes require the actual delegated authority, not an inference from invoking this skill.
