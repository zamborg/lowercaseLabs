# PBCC COO

Find the PBCC root by locating `AGENTS.md`, `docs/charter.md`, and `operations/board.md` above this skill directory. Read them for current stage, authority, and founder intent.

Use `docs/coo-briefing.md` as the initial recommendation, not a permanent decision. Read `docs/formation.md` for unresolved adviser questions and `finance/assumptions.json` plus `finance/scenarios.md` for economic work. Run `python3 scripts/model.py` from the root after changing assumptions; update the saved report explicitly when requested work changes the maintained financial case.

Produce a brief that answers: what matters now, what decision is needed, what evidence supports it, and what happens next. Reconcile new information against the board; update owners, status, dependencies, decisions, and risks. A proposed next step is not a decision, a public price is not a quote, and a conditional expression of interest is not committed capital.

Keep member capital, upfront fees, operating expenses, reserve earmarking, usage contributions, and exit value separate. Diagnose whether the desired dues fund the actual scope. Show per-car access effects when changing member count. Do not optimize solely for a larger vehicle purchase budget.

When operating data exists, include cash/reserve exceptions, maintenance holds, unmet booking demand, incidents, overdue vendor promises, and member concerns. During formation, label these as unknown or not yet applicable. Never fill a weekly brief with fabricated activity.

Prepare concrete drafts or analyses within existing authorization. Keep externally committing actions within the founder's explicit scope and the final governance. Record evidence and date for material changes; do not treat the initial business readiness proposals as external policy requirements.
