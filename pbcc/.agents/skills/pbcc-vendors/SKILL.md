# PBCC vendors

Locate the PBCC root above this skill directory. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/charter.md`, `operations/board.md`, and the relevant sections of `docs/landscape.md`, `docs/formation.md`, and `operations/outreach.md`.

Use current primary provider sources for leads and refresh prices before comparison. Separate public advertisements, direct quotes, founder relationships, and unverified assumptions. Record source/date, validity, scope, exclusions, deposits, minimums, and availability. Do not invent a referral or imply a negotiation occurred.

Compare total costs and practical fit for the same four-car/shared-driver scope:

- Storage: permitted club use, driver access and notice, daily-driver parking, keys/logs, retrieval charges, security, custody insurance, chargers, towing, and exit terms. Dedicated leases also need fit-out, utilities, permitted use, and staffing costs.
- Service: actual marque/generation support, inspection independence, estimate approval, response/turnaround, transport, records, parts/warranty terms, and retainer expiry/refunds. Rate discounts alone do not prove value.
- Insurance: disclosed entity/member/fee model, approved-driver use, actual policy coverage and exclusions, custody/transport gaps, deductibles/value basis, premium and conditions. A club-events product is not evidence of shared-fleet driving coverage.
- Advisers: relevant engagement scope, concrete deliverables, exclusions, fee basis, and dependencies before collecting capital.

For outreach, adapt the maintained draft to confirmed facts and the actual recipient. Default to a prepared draft under current project scope; use any later explicit send or scheduling authorization without requesting it again. Keep negotiations and spending inside that scope, and do not turn a vendor's reply into authority to pay or sign.

Summarize the best option and meaningful tradeoffs, identify missing comparable facts, and update the board's quote/correspondence records with evidence and next action. Keep private correspondence and sensitive documents outside tracked files.
