## Sources and limits

- **Storage per car:** AutoKeep advertises $500/month for multiple indoor cars, checked 2026-11-08: https://www.theautokeep.com/ . Shared-club eligibility, availability and extra fees unconfirmed.
- **Annual entity tax:** California FTB LLC overview: https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/index.html . $800 modeled only if an LLC is selected; other taxes/fees not determined.
- **All other costs:** Unquoted planning inputs. Purchase allocations are ceilings, not vehicle appraisals or current market estimates. Initial management fee and monthly management are separate hypothetical payments, not approved compensation.
- **Tax:** 9% acquisition rate is a sensitivity placeholder. Actual jurisdiction, use classification, and tax on acquisition versus receipts require accountant confirmation; taxes on usage receipts are not modeled.
- **Reserve:** Repair contributions are earmarking of cash, not an external expense. Funding need includes saving this amount; total bank cash decreases only by external cash costs less revenue. Routine maintenance excludes major repairs covered by reserves.
- **Mileage:** 3000 annual miles per car is a planning scenario, not an insurer limit or forecast. Maintenance is a flat placeholder at this scenario's mileage; adjust costs with usage rather than treating extra miles as free.
- **Excluded:** Driver-paid fuel, depreciation/appreciation, capital opportunity cost, debt, major uninsured losses, usage taxes, unquoted lease deposits/fit-out, and professional fees beyond the placeholder allocations. Contingency is budgeted as spent; unspent contingency would improve cash. This is not a full economic return model.

A reduction in quoted costs changes the result. Increasing membership improves dues coverage but also changes access. Usage fees close the gap only after incremental costs and applicable taxes. Capital left in the bank is not a guaranteed member redemption value.
